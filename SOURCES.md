## Technical reading

Lessons contain individual links to MDN developer documentation, ENISA pseudonymisation guidance and EU law. ICO encryption guidance is labelled as UK technical background and is not presented as an authority on EU-law interpretation. Explanations, analogies, scenarios and knowledge checks are original AI-assisted writing, not reproduced source passages.

## Coverage limits and maintenance

The bank contains 7 / 15 / 11 / 10 / 7 questions across domains I–V, respectively. It samples core topics, not every syllabus performance indicator. In particular, further work is needed on case law, national variations, compensation and representative actions, records of processing, restrictions of rights, surveillance jurisprudence, related digital legislation and detailed AI/NIS2 rules. The technology section supports career development and is not an exam-domain checklist.

Each question records an ID, domain, topic, four answer options, a zero-based correct-answer index, explanation, legal reference, URL, original-content provenance and source-check date. Source checking is not independent trainer or legal review. Obtain qualified review and add scenario depth before describing this as comprehensive exam preparation.

Question selection is random within chosen filters and does not apply blueprint weighting. There is no scaled scoring, readiness score or guarantee of passing. Current adequacy decisions and rapidly changing AI rules are deliberately not hard-coded as definitive lists.
